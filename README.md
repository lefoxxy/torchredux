# TorchRedux

Ever put together the perfect Minecraft modpack... only to realize one of your core utility mods never made it to your version?

Yeah. That’s exactly why TorchRedux exists.

TorchRedux is a community-driven continuation of Torcherino(Unofficial), built for the versions that got skipped, left behind, or stuck somewhere between loader changes and abandoned update paths.

If your pack depends on Torcherino-style tick acceleration, you shouldn’t have to rebuild your entire setup just because support stopped.

That’s where TorchRedux comes in.

---

## What is TorchRedux?

TorchRedux brings back the classic Torcherino experience for unsupported NeoForge and Minecraft versions.

That means faster crops. Faster machines. Faster furnaces. Faster testing. Less waiting.

Because honestly... sometimes you just want your farm to grow, your machines to process, and your automation to actually move at a pace that feels good.

TorchRedux helps make that happen.

---

## Why I Made This

I kept running into the same problem.

A modpack would be nearly finished. Everything worked. Everything felt right.

Then I’d hit one issue:

Torcherino wasn’t available for the version I wanted.

Maybe there was an older Forge build. Maybe there was a newer unofficial branch. Maybe support skipped your exact version completely.

Either way, the result was always the same:

Players and pack creators were stuck.

TorchRedux fixes that.

---

## Who Is This For?

TorchRedux is for:

* Players who want less waiting and more playing
* Server owners who need dependable utility mods
* Modpack creators trying to support overlooked versions
* Anyone who refuses to abandon a great pack because one mod got left behind

If that sounds like you... welcome.

---

## What You Get

TorchRedux keeps things simple:

* Classic Torcherino-style acceleration
* NeoForge-focused support
* Lightweight and pack-friendly
* Designed to feel familiar
* Built to fill version gaps, not reinvent the wheel

No unnecessary systems.
No feature bloat.
No trying to become something else.

Just Torcherino... where it should’ve existed in the first place.

---

## The Goal

TorchRedux isn’t about replacing Torcherino.

It’s about making sure great versions don’t get forgotten.

Because sometimes all a modpack needs... is one torch.
